# Sexual Harassment: Overview and Actions

## 1. What behaviors cause sexual harassment?

### Types of Behavior:
- **Verbal**: Sexual comments, jokes, propositions.
- **Non-verbal**: Leering, inappropriate gestures, suggestive images/messages.
- **Physical**: Unwelcome touching, blocking movement, sexual assault.
- **Power Abuse**: Demanding favors for professional benefits, retaliation for rejection.

---

## 2. What to do if you face or witness such behavior?

### Immediate Steps:
- **Set Boundaries**: Tell the harasser their behavior is unacceptable.
- **Document**: Record details (date, time, place, witnesses).

### Seek Support:
- **Report**: Notify HR, Internal Complaints Committee (ICC), or relevant authority.
- **Confide**: Share with a trusted colleague or friend for guidance.

### Formal Action:
- **File Complaint**: Submit a written report as per organizational policy or legal requirements.
- **Contact Law Enforcement**: In cases of criminal behavior.

### Promote Awareness:
- **Educate**: Advocate for awareness of sexual harassment policies.
- **Support**: Stand with victims and create a safe environment.
